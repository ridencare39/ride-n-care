## Capacity notes for one part-time writer
- An SP (service page) uses the existing renderer: the writing work is unique intro, detail paragraphs, FAQs — roughly half a day each.
- B (blog post) 800–1,200 words: one day including internal links.
- A (area uplift) batch of 4: one day if the writer works from the areas.ts data + their own local knowledge; the quality bar (see 03-keyword-url-map.md) bans name-swap templating.
- Owner-input items stall their week — the calendar slots a no-dependency piece alongside each so the week's output never drops below 3.
