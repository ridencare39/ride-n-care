# Ride N Care: 90-Day SEO and Speed Upgrade

## Goal
Improve mobile speed, technical SEO, local visibility, AI-search clarity, and conversion without redesigning the site, changing established URLs, or disrupting booking, call, WhatsApp, and AI booking flows.

## Phase 1 — Immediate implementation

### Performance
- Establish mobile and desktop baselines for the homepage and key bike, car, pricing, service, and area pages.
- Reduce initial page weight and request count by loading only essential shared features at startup.
- Optimize the hero and service imagery for each viewport, keep the main image high priority, and defer below-the-fold images and external media.
- Remove avoidable font latency, animation work, duplicate media loading, and unnecessary third-party requests while preserving the existing appearance.
- Add reduced-motion behavior and stable image/layout dimensions to protect interaction speed and layout stability.

### Technical SEO
- Audit every public content route for a unique title, description, canonical, Open Graph fields, one H1, logical headings, indexability, and correct status behavior.
- Preserve the existing custom-domain sitemap and crawler access, while correcting only verified omissions or inaccurate entries.
- Validate breadcrumbs, internal links, image alternatives, article/service markup, and public AI-readable information.
- Remove or qualify unsupported structured-data claims rather than inventing branches, reviews, ratings, addresses, prices, response times, or operating details.

### Core page and local SEO
- Strengthen homepage, bike, car, service, guide, FAQ, and contact connections using existing helpful content and natural descriptive links.
- Keep bike and car intent distinct and retain the canonical shared pricing/booking source.
- Treat the listed Bangalore localities as service areas only. Do not represent any as a physical centre until verified branch details are supplied.
- Keep the verified Google Business Profile and official phone/WhatsApp links consistent across public business information.

### Mobile and conversion
- Verify 360, 375, 390, 414, and 430px layouts for overflow, tap targets, menus, fixed actions, dialogs, and booking review screens.
- Preserve the existing shared booking architecture and confirm every entry point reaches the correct flow.
- Keep the truthful “Awaiting Confirmation” WhatsApp handoff and prevent duplicate submission where safely possible.
- Prepare privacy-safe GA4 event hooks, but activate tracking only after the owner supplies a valid Measurement ID.

## Phase 2 — Validation and reporting
- Run type checks and browser checks on changed routes and all important booking entry points.
- Re-measure mobile and desktop performance after changes and report only observed values.
- Re-run the SEO foundations review and validate sitemap, robots, metadata, structured data output, internal links, and 404 behavior.
- Deliver a concise report of completed work, unresolved external dependencies, a 90-day owner schedule, and a realistic weekly checklist.

## Owner input required
- GA4 Measurement ID (`G-...`) before analytics can be activated.
- Verified details for each of the eight physical service centres: branch name, full address, public phone, opening hours, and Google Maps link. Until supplied, all locality pages remain accurately labelled as service areas.

## Technical notes
- Existing TanStack routes, canonical URLs, booking functions, pricing data, and phone/WhatsApp numbers remain authoritative.
- No paid tools, new duplicate pages, bulk thin locality/model pages, fabricated reviews, or ranking guarantees will be introduced.
- Live PageSpeed results remain affected by hosting, network conditions, third-party services, and the currently published version; project measurements will be clearly separated from live production measurements.
